Step 2: Installation Guide

Follow these steps to activate the filter on Chrome or Edge:

1. Extract the folder: Find the downloaded `.zip` file, right-click it, and select "Extract All" to unzip it.
2. Open Extensions: In your browser, type chrome://extensions/ (or edge://extensions/) into the top address bar and hit Enter.
3. Enable Developer Mode: Turn on the "Developer mode" switch located in the top right corner (Chrome) or the left menu (Edge).
4. Load the Extension: Click the "Load unpacked" button and select the folder you just extracted.

✅ That's it! Your browser is now locked down and distraction-free.
